The Hat Nobody Could See
Four men buried to the neck. Two black hats, two white, one brick wall between them. Only one man may speak, and a wrong word ends it for all four. So nobody speaks at all - and that silence is exactly how they win. #riddle #brainteaser #puzzle #logicpuzzle #brainheist #quiz
