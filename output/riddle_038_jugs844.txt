Split Eight Liters Without a Mark
A $2,000,000 treasure chest is yours if you split 8 liters of water into exactly 4 and 4. You get three clay jugs that hold 8, 5 and 3 liters, no markings at all, and every pour must fill a jug or empty it. Seven pours do it. #riddle #brainteaser #puzzle #logicpuzzle #brainheist #quiz
