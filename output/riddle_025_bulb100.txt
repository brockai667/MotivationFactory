One Lantern, Ten Prisoners
Ten men in ten cells. One room, one lantern, one chance to speak - and nobody ever meets anybody. The trick almost nobody sees: elect ONE counter, let everyone else signal exactly once, and the lantern quietly counts to ten for you. #riddle #brainteaser #puzzle #logicpuzzle #brainheist #quiz
