One Circle, One Survivor
A hundred people stand in a circle and every second one is thrown out, around and around, until a single person is left. Shrink it to ten and the pattern appears: take the biggest power of two below the crowd, double what's left over, add one. #riddle #brainteaser #puzzle #logicpuzzle #brainheist #quiz
