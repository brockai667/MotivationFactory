Two Wooden Cubes Must Show Every Date From 01 To 31 — 12 Faces, 13 Digits, $2,000,000 On The Line
Carve one digit on each of the 12 faces so the pair shows every date of the month. But 11 and 22 force a 1 and a 2 onto both cubes, and the days 01 to 09 force a 0 onto both — that leaves 6 faces for 7 digits. Twelve faces, thirteen digits, and it still works.
