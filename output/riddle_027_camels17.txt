The Borrowed Camel Trick
A father leaves 17 camels to three sons: half to the eldest, a third to the middle, a ninth to the youngest. No cutting camels. Seventeen splits by none of them. Borrow one camel, and 9 + 6 + 2 = 17, then take yours back. Can you see why it works? #riddle #brainteaser #puzzle #logicpuzzle #brainheist #quiz
